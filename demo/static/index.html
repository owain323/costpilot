What the badges mean
APPROVE

Switch it. The model is known, it has a price, and a cheaper one exists. CostPilot suggests the swap and shows the saving.

KEEP

Leave it. The model is already the cheapest option, or it is not in the price table. Either way there is nothing to change.

NOT PRICED

No price to compute. The model comes from a variable, so CostPilot cannot tell which one runs. It stays untouched.

HUMAN

Your call. The model comes from a shared constant used in many places. Changing it is a bigger decision, so a human reviews it first.

CostPilot never changes your code. Every badge is a suggestion, not an edit.
